# 🤝 Disclaimer

The **AMIFound Multimodal Medical Image Restoration and Enhancement Data Resources** (hereinafter referred to as the “Data Resources”) are compiled from multiple medical imaging datasets that are either publicly released or accessible through an application process. They cover seven imaging modalities: CT, MRI, PET, X-ray, ultrasound, endoscopy, and fundus imaging. In our research, paired high-quality and degraded images were constructed from original medical images.

Guided by the principle of **“giving back to the community that has supported us,”** we hope these data source references and related tools will facilitate research on medical image restoration, enhancement, and artificial intelligence. We sincerely thank the creators, maintainers, and contributors of all original datasets. To respect everyone's rights and foster an open and friendly academic community, we kindly ask users to consider the following terms when accessing or using these resources.
